The manuscript consists of 12 pages in standard IEEE Transactions two-column format, comprising 7 multi-panel figures, 5 benchmark tables, rigorous mathematical theorems, cycle-accurate SystemVerilog hardware co-simulation, and 59 comprehensive citations.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent
\textbf{Motivation and Problem Formulation:}\\
Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check (QLDPC) bivariate-bicycle (BB) codes, pioneered by Bravyi et al. (\textit{Nature} 2024), offer order-of-magnitude reductions in physical qubit overhead compared to topological surface codes. However, translating them into physical quantum processors faces a severe classical decoding bottleneck: their dense, non-local Tanner graphs produce complex 3D space-time Detector Error Models (DEM) with thousands of correlated fault mechanisms. Conventional software decoders (e.g., BP+OSD) require hundreds of microseconds on CPUs, causing catastrophic backlog and buffer overflow under the strict $\sim 1\,\mu\text{s}$ coherence budget of superconducting quantum processors.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\noindent
\textbf{Key Scientific \& Engineering Contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Torus Automorphism Equivariance (Theorem 2):} We prove that 2D torus translations $G = \mathbb{Z}_\ell \times \mathbb{Z}_m$ constitute strict DEM graph automorphisms. Weight tying across automorphism orbits collapses 7,308 fault mechanisms into 203 orbits ($36.0\times$ parameter reduction) on $[[72,12,6]]$ and 14,616 mechanisms into 203 orbits ($72.0\times$ reduction) on $[[144,12,12]]$, guaranteeing mathematical equivariance and eliminating untied neural overfitting.
    \item \textbf{Multiplier-Free 6-Bit Dyadic CSD Architecture (Theorem 3):} We prove dyadic clipping bounds ($[-7.75, +7.75]$, $\Delta=0.25$) and implement all variable- and check-node updates with zero DSP multipliers ($\text{DSP}=0$) using two-term Canonical Signed-Digit (CSD) shift-and-add arithmetic.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Stage Margin Gating \& Streaming Sliding Window:} In a streaming sliding window ($W=3, F=1$), our margin gate $G_3$ routes 75.6\% of syndrome windows with zero logical errors ($P_{L, \text{accept}} = 0.0000$), delivering an end-to-end pipeline error rate of $P_{\text{pipe}} = 1.90 \times 10^{-4}$ at 102.4~ns mean latency (240~ns max), driving queue buffer overflow below $6.0 \times 10^{-5}$ under $1\,\mu\text{s}$ quantum cycles.
    \item \textbf{100-Round Continuous Streaming Memory Survival:} We validate continuous multi-round streaming over $R=100$ measurement rounds ($100\,\mu\text{s}$). Active temporal boundary residual propagation preserves $98.1\%$ logical survival at 100 rounds ($F_{\text{log}} = 0.9981$ at 10~$\mu$s, $0.9812$ at 100~$\mu$s), whereas boundary-agnostic decoding collapses to $22.1\%$ ($4.4\times$ degradation).
    \item \textbf{Real-World IBM Quantum Heron Calibration Benchmark:} Benchmarked against the empirical calibration snapshot of IBM's flagship 133-qubit Heron r2 processor ($p_{\text{2Q}} = 2.1 \times 10^{-3}, \eta = 50.0$), Orbit-Tied QNBP converges in only 3.85 mean iterations---the fastest among all 6 models tested (vs. 9.16 for Classical Min-Sum, 11.86 for Normalized Min-Sum, and 8.86 for BP+OSD-CS-10) with $\text{DSP}=0$.
    \item \textbf{Hardware Synthesis \& 10,000-Shot Co-Simulation Parity:} We synthesize our design in SystemVerilog for AMD UltraScale+ XCKU040 ($f_{\text{clk}} = 250\text{ MHz}$, 28,204 LUTs, 0 DSPs) and verify 10,000-shot bit-exact hardware-software co-simulation parity.
\end{enumerate}
